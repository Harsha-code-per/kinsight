# Code of Conduct

KinSight adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). It applies to project spaces and to people representing the project. Be respectful of differing views, give constructive feedback, and respect others' privacy. Harassment, threats, derogatory comments, and sharing private information without consent violate this policy.

Report conduct concerns through a private contact channel listed by the [repository maintainer](https://github.com/loktrishal-05) or use [GitHub's abuse reporting channel](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse). Maintainers review reports confidentially and use the Covenant's enforcement guidelines to determine a proportionate response.
